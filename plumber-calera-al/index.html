Calera utilities and who owns which pipe

Calera Water Works Board supplies water, and the city runs sewer service at 2273 5th Street (205-668-3880). The city also runs its own Natural Gas Department, so gas questions go to the city rather than a private company. We handle the plumbing on your side, from the meter to your fixtures.

Not sure whether a problem is on the city's side or yours? Check your water meter with every fixture off. If the dial moves, water is leaking somewhere on your property. If a sewer backs up in more than one drain, call us and the city sewer office, because the blockage may be in the main.

We tell you at quoting time whether a job is likely to be inspected.

A note on leaks under soil

The Geological Survey of Alabama says of Shelby County that "this particular area of Shelby County has had a history of sinkhole development," and it lists water or sewer line leaks among the non-geologic causes of ground depressions. We are plumbers, not geologists, so we do not diagnose ground conditions.

The practical takeaway: a leak under soil can undermine the ground around it. If you notice a sudden wet spot, a soft patch, a sunken area in the yard or water pooling near the meter or sewer line, call a plumber. We can test the lines, and if the ground itself seems unstable, call the city or an engineer before anyone digs.

Emergency steps before the plumber gets there

Before We Arrive

Find and close the main

Turn the water main valve to off. It is often near the meter or where the line enters the house. If you cannot find it, call the Calera Water Works Board.

Stay clear of live power

Stay out of rooms where water meets outlets, cords or the panel. Switch off the breaker only if you can do so standing on dry ground.

Clear and protect

Move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the wet zone. Use towels and buckets, and photograph the damage for your insurance claim.
