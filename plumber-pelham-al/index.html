What to do before we arrive

The first ten minutes matter most with water. These four steps protect the house while you wait for an emergency plumber.

Before We Arrive

Find the main shutoff

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. In many homes it sits near the water meter or where the line enters the house. If you cannot find it, tell us on the call.

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, leave that area alone and switch off power only if you can reach the breaker dry. Otherwise call 911 or the electric utility.

Clear the space

Move rugs, boxes and furniture away from the wet area. Lift valuables off the floor and put out towels or buckets to slow the spread.

Emergencies we handle in Pelham, AL

Most late-night calls fall into a short list of problems. Each one has a different first move, and knowing which one you have saves time.

  • A pipe that has split or a joint that has blown apart needs fast burst pipe repair after you close the main valve.
  • A slow, hidden or recurring leak calls for leak detection before anyone opens a wall.
  • Gurgling drains or a toilet that backs up when you run the washer point to a blockage that drain cleaning can clear.
  • A tank that leaks at the base or gives no hot water at midnight is a job for water heater repair.
  • A main line that backs up into the lowest drain in the house may need sewer line repair once a camera shows what is wrong.
